A: PO and invoice trail

  • A1 Job number created Office
  • A2 Budget by cost code Office
  • A3 PO: PO number + job number + cost code (field request, counter and card buys too) Office / Field
  • A4 Delivery checked against packing slip, slip to office Field
  • A5 Vendor invoice matched to PO and slip, then job cost Office
  • A6 Job cost checked Owner-PM
  • A7 Billed to the customer: pay app, then final bill Office
  • A8 Open POs closed, final job cost Office

B: Which address?

  • B1 Captured once at job setup: job-site address AND customer billing address Office
  • B2 Permits use the job-site address Office
  • B3 PO ship-to: job site (or shop), never the customer's office Office
  • B4 Crew goes to the job-site address Field
  • B5 Pay apps and invoices go to the customer's billing address Office
  • B6 Lien waivers name the job-site property Office
  • B7 Equipment and warranties listed under the job-site address (service follows) Office
Service work (maintenance, service calls) follows the same idea but runs differently. Service work: next session.
