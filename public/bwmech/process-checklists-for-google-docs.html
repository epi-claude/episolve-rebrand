1. Bid won: don't forget

From the award to an open job. Nothing gets bought until this page is done.

The contract

  • ☐ Read the contract: scope, exclusions, retainage %, pay-app due date, insurance requirements, schedule — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Signed contract (or letter of intent) and the current drawing set in the job folder — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Signed contract returned to the GC — Office
  • ☐ Insurance certificate sent to the GC, meeting what the contract asks for — Office

Open the job

  • ☐ Job number assigned, before anything is bought [A] — Office
  • ☐ Job-site address entered [B] — Office
  • ☐ Customer billing address entered, as a separate field from the job site [B] — Office
  • ☐ GC contacts on file: PM, super, and who receives the pay app — Office
  • ☐ Customer set up in the books with the right billing address [B] — Office
  • ☐ Tax-exempt certificate on file, if the job is exempt — Office

Budget and turnover

  • ☐ Budget loaded by cost code, from the bid [A] — Office
  • ☐ Bid turned over to the office: what we included, what we excluded — Owner-PM

2. Startup: don't forget

Before and during the first weeks on site.

Paperwork to the GC

  • ☐ Schedule of values sent to the GC and approved — Office
  • ☐ Pay-app cut-off date and format confirmed with the GC (AIA form? their portal?) — Office
  • ☐ Submittals sent; approved before equipment is ordered — Owner-PM
  • ☐ RFI log started — Owner-PM

Buying

  • ☐ Long-lead equipment ordered after approval, with delivery dates — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Every PO shows: PO number, job number, cost code [A] — Office
  • ☐ Every PO shows a ship-to: the job site (or shop), never the customer's office [B] — Office
  • ☐ Permits filed under the job-site address [B] — Office

Getting the crew going

  • ☐ Kickoff with the foreman: drawings, scope, budget hours, start date — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Foreman has the job number, job-site address, site contact and the GC super's number [B] — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Crew sent to the job-site address [B] — Field
  • ☐ Site orientation, safety rules and badging done — Field
  • ☐ Gang box, storage, and where trucks can unload — Field

3. Ongoing: don't forget

The weekly and monthly routine, until the punch list.

Every day / every week: field

  • ☐ Timecards by job and cost code, in before payroll cut-off — Field
  • ☐ Daily report: crew, work done, problems, deliveries — Field
  • ☐ Material requests name the job number and where to drop it [A] — Field
  • ☐ Deliveries counted against the packing slip; slip signed and sent to the office [A] — Field
  • ☐ Extra work written up and signed by the GC super the same day — Field

Every week: Owner-PM and office

  • ☐ Change order priced and signed before the work — Owner-PM
  • ☐ RFIs sent, answers logged — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Job cost check: cost to date vs. budget, hours left [A] — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Vendor invoice matched to its PO and packing slip before it's entered [A] — Office
  • ☐ No PO on the invoice (counter or card buy)? Find the job before paying [A] — Office

Every month

  • ☐ Progress % per schedule-of-values line to the office by the cut-off — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Pay app out on time, with signed change orders only [A] — Office
  • ☐ Pay app sent to the customer's billing address [B] — Office
  • ☐ Lien waivers with the pay app; supplier waivers collected — Office
  • ☐ Payments matched to pay apps; retainage held is tracked — Office
  • ☐ Month closed: job cost agrees with the books — Office

4. Closeout: don't forget

From the punch list to the last dollar collected.

Finish the work

  • ☐ Punch list walked with the GC: one written list — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Every punch item assigned and dated — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Equipment started up; start-up sheets kept — Field
  • ☐ Test and balance done; report received — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Owner's staff trained on the equipment — Field

Closeout package

  • ☐ Red-lines handed in for as-builts — Field
  • ☐ Equipment list: model, serial, and where it sits at the job-site address [B] — Field
  • ☐ O&M manuals, warranties (with start dates), as-builts, attic stock to the GC — Office
  • ☐ Warranty end dates on the calendar (service work follows) — Office

Last dollar

  • ☐ All change orders settled and in the final contract amount — Owner-PM
  • ☐ Final pay app and retainage invoice to the customer's billing address [A] — Office
  • ☐ Final lien waivers, ours and suppliers', naming the job-site property [B] — Office
  • ☐ Open POs closed; last vendor invoices in [A] — Office
  • ☐ Final job cost review: bid vs. final, what we learned — Owner-PM
